## Executive Summary
### Challenges
- Managing multiple, disparate security tools resulted in higher complexity and greater administrative overhead
- Traditional VPNs posed reliability and security limitations, increasing the risk of lateral movement of threats and introducing access bottlenecks for staff
- Limited visibility into encrypted traffic created challenges for protecting sensitive patient data across the organization

### Results & Impact
- Reduces administrative overhead for IT staff, freeing resources for more strategic initiatives that support patient care
- Improves operational efficiency for healthcare providers, accelerating care and ensuring best-in-class patient experiences
- Enhanced visibility and protection system-wide by retiring outdated tools and using zero trust to segment access and mitigate risks
