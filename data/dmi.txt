## Executive Summary
### Challenges
- Installing new hardware onto a legacy environment introduced downtime, caused outage windows, and required regular updates
- Requiring users to work from DMI devices made employees less productive and negatively impacted the organization’s global carbon footprint

### Results & Impact
- Deploys zero trust within 2 weeks with zero impact to users and zero downtime
- Saves $700,000 annually, improves onboarding and offboarding experiences, and shortens time to set up new offices and devices
