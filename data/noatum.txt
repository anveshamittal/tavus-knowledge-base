## Executive Summary
### Challenges
- Traditional VPNs left the organization too exposed to cyberattacks when users would access the internet
- Legacy security such as firewalls left the organization unable to inspect encrypted traffic
- Perimeter-based architectures made M&A onboarding take much longer than it should

### Results & Impact
- Enables work-from-anywhere with confidence with secure, seamless user access
- Minimizes user incidents and improves root cause analysis, delivering knowledge and agility
- Improves risk evaluation as well as threat defense by hiding systems and apps from the internet
