## Executive Summary
### Challenges
- An outdated security infrastructure could not support remote working and contributed to operational inefficiencies
- Traditional VPN appliances extended the corporate network to end user devices, putting sensitive data at risk of compromise
- Legacy security point products limited visibility around threats, making risk assessment and mitigation more challenging

### Results & Impact
- Zero trust architecture enhances security posture—processes ~3B transactions and blocks 200k+ threats monthly
- Improves the remote user experience for 15,000 users, and seamlessly integrates with existing identity solutions
- Enables a more comprehensive focus on risk management, driven by better insights into risk factors and security posture
