## Customer Case Study
### Overview
Zscaler Japan K.K., the Japanese subsidiary of Zscaler (NASDAQ: ZS, headquartered in California, USA), a leader in cloud security, announced that together with Daiwa Institute of Research, Ltd. (DIR), it has completed deployment of its zero trust security solution Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) at Daiwa Securities Co., Ltd. (Daiwa Securities) and has decided to deploy Zscaler Private Access (ZPA).

ZIA is a cloud-based solution that provides centrally managed, multilayered defense for internet traffic from various locations and a wide range of devices. It offers multiple security functions, including URL filtering, antivirus, content inspection, and sandboxing, enabling integrated implementation and management of internet access security regardless of location or device.

As supply chains expand through mutual use of public cloud services and partner company systems, cybercrime is becoming more sophisticated, including "supply chain attacks" that impersonate partner companies. Since Covid-19, the spread of remote work among Daiwa Securities employees has increased access to diverse services from various locations and devices. To address these trends, Daiwa Securities decided to deploy ZIA to strengthen security for communications outside the company, such as to public cloud services, covering the business devices employees use in offices, branches, homes, and elsewhere: 2-in-1 devices (12,000) and smartphones (7,000). Implementation of ZIA was completed in March of this year.

The main results Daiwa Securities has achieved so far through the ZIA deployment are as follows:

- Secure, best-path internet access from both inside and outside the company
- High-volume traffic control: controlling 90 million transactions and 4 TB of traffic per day
- Improved capability and accuracy in responding to external threat detection

Daiwa Institute of Research is tackling Daiwa Securities' security enhancement in two phases. Phase 1 corresponds to the ZIA deployment described above. In Phase 2, it will use Zscaler's Zscaler Private Access (ZPA) to strengthen the security of communications to servers and applications on-premises and in the cloud. ZPA is a cloud-delivered ZTNA service that provides secure access to all private applications without the need for a remote access VPN. Implementation of ZPA is scheduled for completion in March 2023. Through the ZPA deployment, the aim is to eliminate the attack surface, accelerate the shift to the cloud, and enable collaboration with business partner companies. Ultimately, by realizing an all-internet zero trust security strategy that does not depend on leased lines, the project will reduce cost and complexity and contribute to greater business agility.
