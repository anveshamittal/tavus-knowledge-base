## Executive Summary
### Challenges

- Traditional VPN gateways created traffic bottlenecks, leading to a poor user experience
- Legacy security tools offered limited insight into browsing activity across the user base

### Results & Impact

- Stays ahead of emerging threats with comprehensive DLP, private access, and zero trust segmentation
- Strengthens enterprise security posture while helping accelerate business with a cloud-first approach
