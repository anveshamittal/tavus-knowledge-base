# summary

phased_journey:

## challenges

- The complex, disparate security infrastructure, including VPN and network access control (NAC), created administrative challenges for the small team of five and resulted in poor performance and security gaps
- Concerned about avoiding data breaches and meeting compliance demands, the organization needed more robust and comprehensive data protection
- The traditional architecture lacked agility and scalability, hindering expansion into new markets and slowing M&A integration

## results

- Creates a consistent, secure, and reliable experience for users at headquarters and branches anytime and on any device
- Protects sensitive data everywhere at rest, in use, and in motion to build customer trust while maintaining compliance with finserv and regional privacy regulations
- Reduces the operational burden and supports growth by consolidating the technology stack on a single, scalable zero trust platform
