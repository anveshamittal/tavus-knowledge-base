# summary

phased_journey:

## challenges

- Radiologists working remotely experienced frustrating performance delays when using the high-latency VPN to access essential medical imaging systems
- A lack of remediation support and recommendations around VPN configuration did not address Med Center Health’s needs
- The IT staff had no visibility into connectivity issues and were unable to find and fix problems in a timely manner

## results

- Provides a high-performance, reliable user experience, doubling remote radiologist productivity and resolving issues faster, increasing revenue potential by approximately $2M/year
- Paves the way for expanding the zero trust platform to protect patient health data, ensure HIPAA compliance, and further reduce the attack surface by eliminating VPN for third-party vendors
