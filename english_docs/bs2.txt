# summary

phased_journey:
[]

## challenges

- Legacy VPN and firewall policies were not scalable and created complexity for teams managing remote and in-office environments
- Inbound exposure for app access expanded the attack surface and heightened the risk of east-west lateral movement
- Limited visibility into user experience and app usage slowed issue resolution and obscured opportunities to optimize spend

## results

- Reduces risk by eliminating VPNs and decreasing reliance on firewalls—removing exposed inbound services and preventing lateral movement through app-level, policy-based access.
- Accelerates M&A by onboarding an acquired company in one week, enabled by Zscaler’s single agent and centralized policies that simplify integration and provide fast, secure access to core apps.
- Empowers IT to optimize spend and resource allocation, leveraging AI-powered insights to resolve issues faster and focus on opportunities to save money for the business.
