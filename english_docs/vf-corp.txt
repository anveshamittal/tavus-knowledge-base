The advantage of prior Zscaler experience

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- I started working with Zscaler 9 years ago when I was at AutoNation, the nation’s largest seller of cars, with over 300 dealerships.
- We rolled out the Zscaler platform to transform our network from hundreds of little stacks of iron in our stores and traditional firewall and VPN-based security.
- This helped us gain robust visibility across all our stores and corporate locations, dramatically decrease our costs, and improve protections for our associates and our consumers.
- We also closed the digital divide between the in-dealership experience and our online experience.

Paragraph 2

- Zscaler has been innovating like no one else, and its Zero Trust Exchange platform goes far beyond SSE for Users.
- Zscaler’s platform protects not just your users, but also your cloud workloads, your OT/IoT devices - like point of sales terminals and diagnostics tablets - and your business associates, suppliers, and consumers.
