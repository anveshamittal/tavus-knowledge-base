## challenges

- A traditional, perimeter-based architecture reliant on data centers could not support accelerated digital transformation
- Legacy firewalls and VPNs lacked the agility to scale with an increase in remote work, putting users and data at risk
- Previous security point products lacked advanced threat detection capabilities, exposing a wider attack surface

## results

- Enables 80,000 employees to work safely from any location and secures remote access for 2,000+ critical private apps
- Reduces architecture complexity and cost—zero firewalls needed at airports and six security point products eliminated
- Unifies the security ecosystem and dynamically enforces policies to block 40% more threats and improve security posture
