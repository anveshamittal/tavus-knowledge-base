# summary

phased_journey:

## challenges

- A traditional security infrastructure was not compatible with a cloud-first approach, putting users and workloads at risk
- Legacy security appliances did not support work-from-anywhere flexibility, resulting in latency and poor performance
- VPNs did not enable identity-based access for private apps, leading to inconsistent policies and a wider attack surface

## results

- Secures outbound and inbound connectivity for a hybrid workforce, delivering consistent protection at every location
- Reduces latency and enhances SaaS and private application performance to improve in-office and remote user experiences
- Streamlines security architecture and delivers comprehensive protection against threats while reducing security spend

Raiffeisen Bank International also known as RBI regards Austria as the leading corporate investment bank, as well as Central and Eastern Europe, we decided to transform our old legacy structure by applying the zero trust architectural principles.

4.

5.

7.

8.
After implementing Private Service Edges we saw a huge improvement in, latency and also in performance of of applications that we had problems with before.

9.
