## challenges

- Defending sensitive data and core internal applications from breaches and data loss was the overriding priority
- Legacy on-premises proxies and traditional firewalls were ineffective in blocking advanced attacks
- Lack of clear visibility into data usage, vulnerabilities, and overall risk profile increased risk

## results

- Maintains granular visibility into internal and external data sharing
- Reduces the organization’s risk score and strengthens its security posture
- Ensures every connection is authorized and secure by authenticating user identity and device health before granting access
