## challenges

- Simplify secure remote access and shrink time to productivity for employees of newly acquired companies

## results

- Deployed zero trust work-from-anywhere access globally within weeks
- Grants new M&A employees access to apps in hours instead of weeks
- Filtered approximately 3.5 billion events in one year
- Prevented more than 745,200 policy violations
- Blocked 37,000+ threats, including 5,490 encrypted threats
- Simplifies remote access for both IT and users
- Transformed network access strategy to zero trust
