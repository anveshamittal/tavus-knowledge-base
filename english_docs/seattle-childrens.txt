# summary

## challenges

- Likelihood of cyber incidents disrupting hospital system operations and impacting patient care called for risk reduction
- Risk from threats hidden in encrypted traffic, driven by a broad user base across four states, raised major concerns
- The hospital needed to partner with a zero trust leader that fully understands the healthcare sector's security requirements

## results

- Thwarts bad actors by creating direct user-to-app connections, making the network invisible to attackers and unauthorized users
- Reduces the attack surface as well as detects and remediates risks with real-time traffic monitoring, offering peace of mind
- Assures the board of directors of the hospital's strong, resilient security posture, ensuring continuous delivery of exceptional care
