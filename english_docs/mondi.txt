Section 1

title:
Fast growth by acquisition increases risk exposure

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- With rapid growth through mergers and acquisitions, Mondi recognized that its legacy hub-and-spoke architecture, which backhauled internet traffic over MPLS to two centralized gateways, no longer met business requirements.
- It was expensive and caused application latency, negatively impacting user experience.
- As part of a decentralization strategy for all of its worldwide locations, Mondi sought a more scalable, better solution with increased security.

Paragraph 2

- The security of paper and packaging businesses are not as advanced as financial institutions, so the state of security at acquired companies is often “highly questionable” and needs a “wide range of clean up,” according to Thomas Vavra, Manager of Communication Networks at Mondi.
- Stopping internet-borne threats from attacking via newly acquired companies was a top priority before integrating new companies into the fold.

Paragraph 3

- Mondi also had a hardware-based proxy, software-based URL filter, and a large number of firewall clusters, all managed by a small IT team.
- However, it needed a less complex solution that would considerably reduce the work required to provide secure internet and SaaS access for Mondi’s growing employee base.

Section 2

id:
improving-user-experience-and-security-posture

title:
Improving user experience and security posture

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- After intensive testing, Mondi deployed an SD-WAN and the Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange to enable local internet breakouts across the globe.
- The company also implemented the Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) service to replace many management-intensive products and help decentralize its network infrastructure.
- ZIA provides fast, direct, secure access to the internet and SaaS applications.

Paragraph 2

- Turning to Zscaler for direct internet access considerably reduced the company’s bandwidth and latency issues—increasing available bandwidth by up to 15 times.
- “This is important since the majority of everyday work today is handled over the internet,” notes Vavra.
- “With Zscaler, our users could access information more quickly as well as more securely.”

Paragraph 3

- “Zscaler helps us clean up the security of newly acquired companies,” continued Vavra.
- “It is like a litmus test with all sorts of security measures built in.
- With Zscaler, we’ve seen a dramatic improvement in security posture.”

title:
Cutting time, costs and supporting cloud transformation

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- In addition to providing better bandwidth and security and easy scalability, implementing the Zero Trust Exchange allowed the company to avoid an MPLS upgrade and eliminate multiple management-intensive products.
- Thus, the investment in Zscaler reduced Mondi’s MPLS costs as well as time spent by security operations managing internet security.

Paragraph 2

- “Zscaler supports Mondi in its digital transformation strategy,” noted Vavra.
- “Through their cloud approach, we were able to not only adapt infrastructure but reinforce security as well.
- Today, we cover the different security functions from the proxy to the firewall using an approach that provides a high level of security and extremely transparent administration.”
