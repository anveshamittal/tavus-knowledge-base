# summary

## challenges

- Legacy VPNs had limited flexibility and could not scale with Unilever’s global cloud strategy
- Traditional security model increased risk due to insufficient access control and visibility
- Rising demand for remote access strained the VPN infrastructure, impacting the user experience

## results

- Reduces risk with secure, direct access to applications and without the limitations and vulnerabilities of VPN
- Enhances operational efficiency by processing large-scale data traffic with 99.99% uptime
- Supports global cloud strategy by providing secure remote access across 190 countries, maintaining flexibility for Unilever’s workforce
