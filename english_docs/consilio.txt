# summary

## challenges

- After a series of outages from major VPN providers, leadership mandated a transition from legacy remote access VPNs to a modern zero trust network access solution
- Integrating M&A systems under a castle-and-moat architecture introduced operational complexity that ultimately slowed time to realize value
- Using VDI to provide access to Consilio’s third-party review attorneys was costly, unreliable, and time-consuming for IT to maintain

## phased_journey

- Replaced VPNs to secure application access, choosing to expand with Zscaler after successful internet access initiative
- Provided safe, zero trust connectivity for acquired systems and users with Zscaler Private Access and Cloud Browser Isolation
- Switched from VDI to a cloud native, agentless approach to secure application access on review attorneys’ unmanaged devices

## results

- Mitigates risk with a modern zero trust architecture that minimizes the attack surface and strengthens Consilio’s overall security posture
- Optimizes user experiences via faster, reliable connectivity and simple onboarding for employees, M&A users, and third parties
- Reduces cost and administrative overhead through a single, multipurpose platform with one administrative interface to deploy global policy updates at the click of a button
