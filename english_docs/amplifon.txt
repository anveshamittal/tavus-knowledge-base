Zscaler enables safer internet connections worldwide

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- An on-premises, legacy proxy solution no longer provided the capacity or scalability Amplifon needed to support thousands of geographically dispersed hearing care centers—or its plans for continued expansion.

Paragraph 2

- Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) enables secure, reliable, and fast connections to the internet and SaaS applications across Amplifon locations.
- Powered by the world’s largest inline security cloud, the Zero Trust Exchange displaces traditional proxy appliances as a more secure way to broker direct-to-internet access for users worldwide.

Paragraph 3

- With cloud firewall, URL filtering, and TLS/SSL inspection built into the comprehensive Zscaler platform, multiple point products are no longer necessary.
- In addition, by delivering security from the cloud, as close to the user as possible, Zscaler eliminates the need to backhaul internet traffic to a data center.

Paragraph 4

- For Amplifon, this means that even with a streamlined outbound security stack, they benefit from more rigorous security across their global operations, and every user benefits from a consistent and seamless connectivity experience.

Paragraph 5

- “With Zscaler, we are managing safer access to the internet and public applications on a global scale.
- Every user can be securely connected from anywhere,” said Mallen.

Section 4

id:
zscaler-delivers-zero-trust-access-to-keep-patient-data-secure

title:
Zscaler delivers zero trust access to keep patient data secure

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- Amplifon supports millions of patients worldwide with a team of more than 10,000 hearing care professionals, requiring secure management of highly sensitive patient data and other essential private resources.

Paragraph 2

- Before Zscaler, Amplifon used client-to-site virtual private network (VPN) appliances for managing inbound security.
- Traditional VPN appliances inherently create a wider attack surface.

Paragraph 3

- Zscaler Private Access (ZPA) provides least-privileged access to Amplifon private applications on a need-to-know basis only.
- Private resources are accessed directly through the Zero Trust Exchange, never exposed to the internet.

Paragraph 4

- Zscaler verifies user identity, device security posture, and context before an inbound connection is made while inline traffic inspection enforces access control policies and actively prevents web attacks.
- This dramatically minimizes the attack surface and eliminates lateral movement if a bad actor gains access to the network.

Paragraph 5

- “Private Amplifon applications—and importantly, patient data—are always protected behind the Zero Trust Exchange,” shared Mallen.
- “Zscaler ensures that our users have direct access to the data they need while allowing us to define and enforce access control policies on a very granular level across the globe.”

leveraging-zscaler-platform-for-safer-faster-m-a-integration

title:
Leveraging Zscaler platform for safer, faster M&A integration

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- Amplifon has been expanding global operations through strategic acquisitions since the 1990s.
- Today, the company operates across EMEA, the Americas, and APAC, and corporate growth is not slowing.

Paragraph 2

- Zscaler simplifies integrating acquired companies into the Amplifon security infrastructure for Mallen.
- The Zero Trust Exchange can automatically handle expanding volumes of traffic and users at scale without intensive manual intervention or increased risk to the corporate network.

Paragraph 3

- Before Zscaler, Mallen would create a standard site-to-site VPN to connect a newly acquired call center network to the Amplifon corporate network.
- This process could take up to 30 days and risk a wider attack surface.
- On the Zero Trust Exchange, Mallen simply needs to grant new users zero trust connectivity to relevant private applications, along with installing Zscaler Client Connector™ on their devices.
- This new process can be completed in roughly three days—90% faster than before.

Paragraph 4

- “Amplifon is growing at a rapid pace, but we are able to leverage the Zscaler solution to accelerate ongoing M&A activity,” said Mallen.
- “The scalability of the Zero Trust Exchange gives us flexibility to expand as a business, efficiently and with agility, while maintaining our secure edge.”
