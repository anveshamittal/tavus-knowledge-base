## challenges

- Separate business units meant separate means of security without a cloud-based model
- VPNs created tedious process of authenticating users with lackluster security
- Partner offices could not connect to data centers and could not access applications

## results

- Secures 12,000-15,000 users in 2.5 months with Zscaler Internet Access
- Enables work-from-anywhere, enabling flexible, agile business with an office-like experience
- Delivers secure access to workloads and applications
