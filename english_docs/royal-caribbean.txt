## challenges

- Become a more secure enterprise, decrease operational costs, and provide a better user experience

## results

- Improves security posture by reducing reliance on hub-and-spoke networks
- Delivers seamless experiences to whichever location(s) employees work from
- Bolsters cyber defenses and threat detection with zero trust security
- Lowers operational costs by simplifying data centers and lessening the need for appliances
