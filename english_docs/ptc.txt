## challenges

- Legacy VPN enabled threat actors by exposing internal IP addresses, allowing lateral threat movement across the network
- Increased adoption of cloud services and SaaS necessitated a policy-based approach to connect and secure apps, identities, and devices
- Fragmented and limited visibility into network issues resulted in excessive time spent on troubleshooting and diagnosis

## results

- Enables work from anywhere with a café-like experience, offering least-privileged access to resources without putting users on the network
- Reduces the attack surface by replacing vulnerable infrastructure and blocking threats regardless of user location or device
- Saves money by reducing upgrades and maintenance of an aging cybersecurity physical infrastructure
