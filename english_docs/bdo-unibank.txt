# summary

phased_journey:

## challenges

- Transitioning from an on-premises data center to SaaS apps and the cloud drove a need for a secure remote access solution for employees across all branches
- Over-privileged access to apps and inadequate monitoring tools increased risk and negatively impacted the bank’s security posture
- The CIO and his team sought a scalable platform that would support today’s needs and future expansion as the bank’s requirements mature

## results

- Reduces risk by ensuring staff get the right level of access only to authorized apps and resources
- Improves the bank’s security posture with continuous inspection of all web traffic, including TLS/SSL, user activity monitoring, and AI-powered advanced threat protection
- Eases the burden on IT by automating policy enforcement and provisioning—all managed from a single, unified platform
