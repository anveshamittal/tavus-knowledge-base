- Supports the microsegmented, policy-based private application access policies not possible with traditional VPNs

Section 3

zscaler-bridges-the-gaps-left-by-legacy-security-solutions

title:
Zscaler bridges the gaps left by legacy security solutions

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- Deploying Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) as part of the Zero Trust Exchange has helped Colt reach a critical, initial benchmark on its zero trust journey: replacing an aging proxy solution with direct, secure access to the internet and SaaS applications—powered by the world’s largest inline security cloud.

Paragraph 2

- Proxy appliances present limitations when it comes to supporting secure outbound connections.
- Capacity and scalability issues curtail complete inspection of encrypted traffic, resulting in blind spots to malware.
- Zscaler bridges these gaps inherent in traditional legacy solutions.

Paragraph 3

- The Zero Trust Exchange enables Colt to inspect all encrypted traffic at scale to stop threats and data loss in real time.
- By delivering security through the cloud, as close to the end user as possible, Zscaler also eliminates the need to backhaul internet traffic to a distant data center, reducing potential choke points.

Paragraph 4

- Colt employees now enjoy direct-to-internet access from anywhere, boosting connectivity and productivity.
- Surti appreciates the peace of mind that comes with knowing this access is backed by comprehensive zero trust security.

Paragraph 5

- “The Zscaler platform gives my security team greater confidence that our workforce can perform their roles safely with fewer limitations on their productivity,” said Surti.

Achieving a better security posture faster with Zscaler

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- After collaborating with Zscaler as a partner to help their own customers leverage the Zero Trust Exchange, Colt anticipated a fast deployment.

Section 7

id:
securing-private-applications-with-zscaler

title:
Securing private applications with Zscaler

### paragraphs

Paragraph 1

- Colt is already actively planning for the next step on its zero trust journey.
- The company will displace its legacy VPN appliances and deploy Zscaler Private Access (ZPA) to enable fast, seamless, zero trust access to private applications.

Paragraph 2

- With Zscaler, private applications are hidden behind the Zero Trust Exchange, not left exposed to the internet; users are connected directly only to the private applications they need and not to the corporate network as a whole.
- This reduces the attack surface and limits lateral threat movement, respectively.
- Additionally, Zscaler verifies user identity, device security posture, and context before an inbound connection is made, while inline traffic inspection enforces access control policies and actively prevents web attacks.

Paragraph 3

- “The Zscaler platform removes the risk of VPNs altogether, while further improving the remote user experience at Colt,” said Surti.
- “With Zscaler, we can manage private application and data access more dynamically and granularly while at the same time simplifying the process of securely connecting employees.
