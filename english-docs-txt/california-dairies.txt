=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Established in 1999, California Dairies is a leading manufacturer of butter, milk powder, nutritional milk powder, and fluid milk products. It is co-owned by nearly 300 dairy families and located throughout California’s rural landscape from San Diego County to Sacramento County.
Industry: Food, Beverage, and Tobacco
Headquarters: Visalia, California, USA
Company Size: 400 farms, ships products to 50 countries
Source URL: https://www.zscaler.com/customers/california-dairies

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Challenges:
  * Move from on-premises to cloud to improve accessibility, flexibility, and security, primarily over OT environments
Key Results:
  * Transitions to a primarily cloud-based environment to gain flexibility
  * Improves access control with privileged remote access capabilities
  * Reduces reliance on legacy hardware by eliminating need for perimeter-based security
  * Increases efficiency by lessening the need for manual configurations across IT/OT environments
