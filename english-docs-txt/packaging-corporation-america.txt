=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Headquartered in Lake Forest, Illinois, Packaging Corporation of America is one of the largest producers of containerboard and corrugated packaging products in the U.S., producing boxes and displays for grocery stores. The company has over 15,000 employees and over 70 factories.
Industry: Manufacturing
Headquarters: Lake Forest, IL, USA
Company Size: 15,000+ employees
Source URL: https://www.zscaler.com/customers/packaging-corporation-america

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Challenges:
  * Establish secure connectivity after moving from a centralized egress point to distributed egress points across 70+ plant locations
Key Results:
  * Secures internet access at 70+ plant locations
  * Simplifies IT management by limiting configuration changes with a cloud-based solution
  * Defends against known and unknown malware variants
  * Gains a holistic view of the network environment
