=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
CSM Ingredients is the leading supplier of the highest quality ingredients with a wide range across bakery fats, pastry, and bread ingredients as well as food solutions for multiple industrial applications in bakery, dairy, non-dairy, and the ice cream industry.
Industry: Food, Beverage, and Tobacco
Headquarters: Atlanta, Georgia, USA
Company Size: 39 factories in 100 countries
Source URL: https://www.zscaler.com/customers/csm-ingredients

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Challenges:
  * Fix control problems stemming from legacy VPN through a network redesign that begins in the cloud
Key Results:
  * Implemented Active Directory authentication after ditching VPN
  * Simplifies access and reduces risk for third-party vendors
  * Streamlines on- and offboarding with a straightforward authentication dashboard
  * Reduces reliance on hardware- and appliance-based security
