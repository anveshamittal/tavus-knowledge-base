=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Challenges:
  * The shift from a hospital-centric to an ambulatory model of care meant a more distributed, diffuse ecosystem that was difficult to consistently protect.
  * Perimeter-based security tools were complex and costly to manage, limiting AdventHealth’s ability to securely scale alongside its rapid growth.
  * Limited visibility into device and user activity hampered compliance, slowed incident response, and hindered proactive risk management.
Key Results:
  * Simplifies network architecture, accelerating the onboarding of new facilities and ambulatory sites.
  * Decreases the blast radius of threats through meaningful segmentation and by removing users from the network.
  * Evolves exposure and risk management through real-time, dynamic visibility device activity, user behavior, and exposures.
