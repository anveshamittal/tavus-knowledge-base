=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Reckitt Benckiser Group plc is a British multinational consumer goods company headquartered in Slough, England. It is a producer of health, hygiene and nutrition products. The company was formed in March 1999 by the merger of British company Reckitt & Colman plc and Dutch company Benckiser N.V..
Industry: Manufacturing
Headquarters: Slough, Great Britain, UK
Company Size: 43,000+ employees
