=== COMPANY OVERVIEW ===
Founded in 1961 in Louisville, Kentucky, Humana is a for-profit health insurance company. With more than 95,500 employees as of December 2021, they offer a simple approach—offer personalized care from people who care.
Industry: Healthcare and Pharmaceutical
Headquarters: Louisville, KY, USA
Company Size: 95,500+ employees
Source URL: https://www.zscaler.com/customers/humana

=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Challenges:
  * Deliver zero touch security to ensuring great experiences while supporting a remote workforce
Key Results:
  * Deployed ZPA to 10,000 users—with plans to deploy to all users
  * Enables work-from-anywhere across 75% of the workforce
  * Uses Cloud Browser to simplify third-party authentication and reduce risk
  * Simplifies onboarding by leveraging Zscaler and Azure AD together
  * Replaces VPN to improve security and the overall experience
