=== EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ===
Enterprise AI usage has reached a tipping point, growing from experimental pilots to a foundational layer of modern business operations. Between January and December 2025, Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange logged over 989.3 billion AI/ML transactions across global enterprises.
However, rapid adoption has created significant security challenges, including shadow AI usage, sensitive data loss, embedded AI exposures in SaaS platforms, and GenAI-enhanced threat actor campaigns.

Key Takeaways:
  * Total AI/ML transactions reached 989.3 billion in 2025.
  * Grammarly and ChatGPT lead enterprise transaction volume.
  * ChatGPT accounts for the majority of DLP policy violations (410M+ violations).
  * DPRK-linked threat actors are actively leveraging GenAI for social engineering lures and malware development.
  * Zscaler Red Teaming revealed 90% of enterprise AI systems fail under adversarial probes within 1h 27m.
  * AI/ML activity increased 83.3% year-over-year.
  * Data transfer volumes to AI applications rose 92.6%, reaching more than 18,000 TB.
  * Organizations blocked 39% of AI/ML transactions due to policy and data exposure concerns.

Key Statistics:
  * Total AI/ML transactions: 989.3 billion (2025)
  * ChatGPT DLP policy violations: 410,181,006 (2025)
  * Median time to critical AI system failure in Red Teaming: 16 minutes (2025)
  * AI/ML activity year-over-year growth: 83.3% (2024-2025)
  * Data transfer volume growth to AI applications: 92.6% (2024-2025)
  * Data transferred to AI applications: 18,000 TB (2025)
  * AI/ML transactions blocked: 39% (2025)

=== KEY FINDINGS ===
- AI transaction volume grew exponentially to 989.3 billion: Zscaler cloud telemetry logged over 989.3 billion AI/ML transactions globally, representing a massive shift in how employees interact with AI tools in daily workflows.
- Grammarly, ChatGPT, and Codeium lead enterprise AI adoption: Transaction volume is heavily concentrated among top productivity and coding tools: Grammarly (327.3B transactions), ChatGPT (120.2B transactions), and Codeium (42.3B transactions).
- ChatGPT accounts for the vast majority of AI DLP policy violations: Data loss prevention telemetry recorded 410,181,006 DLP policy violations for ChatGPT, followed by Codeium (242,263,311) and GitHub Copilot (31,223,009).
- Embedded AI capabilities in SaaS tools introduce silent risk: Features embedded directly in standard SaaS applications invoke AI automatically, sharing enterprise content without dedicated AI application visibility or governance.
- DPRK-linked threat actors weaponize GenAI for malware and lures: Campaigns such as Contagious Interview leverage GenAI to draft hyper-realistic recruitment messages and refine malicious payloads like BeaverTail and InvisibleFerret.
- 90% of enterprise AI systems fail under adversarial red teaming in under 1.5 hours: Empirical exploit data from Zscaler red teaming across 25+ enterprise environments showed a median time to first critical failure of 16 minutes, with probe failure rates led by Bias (49%) and Off Topic (47%).
